# Sources

## Model capability and forecasting

- [OpenAI GPT-5.6 launch and evaluation tables](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/)
- [GPT-5.6 Sol model documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-sol)
- [Halawi et al., Approaching Human-Level Forecasting with Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18563)
- [ForecastBench paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839) and [benchmark documentation](https://www.forecastbench.org/docs/)
- [Lopez-Lira and Tang, Can ChatGPT Forecast Stock Price Movements?](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X26001066)
- [Levy, numerical reasoning and look-ahead bias](https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-679x.70058)
- [Moore et al., confidence calibration](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2016.2525)

OpenAI publishes strong finance-research, browsing, science, and professional-work evaluations for GPT-5.6 Sol. It does not publish a stock-direction hit rate, Brier score on earnings events, trading P&L, or Sharpe ratio. Benchmark percentages must never be inserted into Kelly sizing as though they were market forecasts.
